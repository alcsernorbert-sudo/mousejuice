MountJuice

Keeps your network shares mounted · Mac App Store

Support

Questions or problems? Write to alcsernorbert@gmail.com. Please include your macOS version, the protocol (SMB, AFP, NFS, WebDAV or FTP) and the lines from Activity Log in the MountJuice menu.

  • Shares in /Volumes: MountJuice mounts to /Volumes when macOS allows it. Otherwise it uses its own MountJuice Volumes folder; use Show in Finder to open the share.
  • NFS and WebDAV are mounted through the Finder, because macOS does not allow App Store apps to mount them directly.
  • Wi-Fi rules need Location Services permission: macOS only tells apps the Wi-Fi name when they have it.
  • Scripts go into ~/Library/Application Scripts/hu.alcser.MountJuice/. Arguments: event, share name, mount point, share address.

Privacy

MountJuice does not collect, store or send any personal data. There are no analytics, no ads and no accounts.

  • Your share settings are stored only on your Mac. Passwords are stored in your macOS keychain and are never exported.
  • MountJuice connects only to the servers you configure (and your local network, to find servers and send Wake-on-LAN packets).
  • Location permission is used only to read the current Wi-Fi network name for Wi-Fi rules. Your location is never stored or sent.

Magyarul

Támogatás: írj a alcsernorbert@gmail.com címre; küldd el a macOS verzióját, a protokollt és az Eseménynapló sorait.

Adatvédelem: a MountJuice semmilyen személyes adatot nem gyűjt, nem tárol és nem küld el. A beállítások csak a Maceden vannak, a jelszavakat a kulcskarikád tárolja. A helymeghatározási engedélyt csak a Wi-Fi-hálózat nevének kiolvasására használja.
