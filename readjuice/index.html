ReadJuice

RSS reader and read-later app for Mac, iPhone and iPad · one-time purchase, no subscription

Support

Questions or problems? Write to alcsernorbert@gmail.com. Please include your device, its system version and, for a feed that doesn't load, its address.

  • Sync: feeds you add “On My Devices” sync through your own iCloud account. Sign in to the same Apple Account on every device.
  • Feed services: ReadJuice works with Feedbin, Feedly, Inoreader, FreshRSS, BazQux Reader, The Old Reader, Miniflux, Fever, NewsBlur and Tiny Tiny RSS. For FreshRSS turn on API access and use your API password; for Tiny Tiny RSS turn on “Enable API”; for Feedly paste a developer access token.
  • Read later: save pages to ReadJuice itself or to Instapaper, wallabag or Pinboard.
  • Moving from another reader: export your subscriptions as OPML and choose File › Import Subscriptions (OPML).

Privacy

ReadJuice does not collect any data. There are no analytics, no ads, no accounts and no servers of ours.

  • Your feeds, articles, read status and saved pages are stored on your devices and in your own private iCloud database. The developer has no access to them.
  • ReadJuice downloads feeds and web pages directly from the sites you subscribe to, and talks directly to the feed or read-later services you sign in to. Those sites and services receive the requests as with any web browser.
  • Passwords and tokens for services are kept in your Keychain.
  • Article summaries are made on your device with Apple Intelligence; the text does not leave your device.

Magyarul

Támogatás: írj az alcsernorbert@gmail.com címre. Más olvasóból: exportáld a feliratkozásaidat OPML-be, majd a ReadJuice-ban válaszd a Fájl › Feliratkozások importálása (OPML) menüt.

Adatvédelem: a ReadJuice semmilyen adatot nem gyűjt. A feedjeid, cikkeid és mentett oldalaid az eszközeiden és a saját iCloud-fiókodban vannak, a fejlesztő nem fér hozzájuk. Az app közvetlenül a feliratkozott oldalakról és a bejelentkezett szolgáltatásokból tölt le; a jelszavakat a Kulcskarika őrzi. Az AI-összefoglaló az eszközön készül.
